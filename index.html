Sunapee Sound Project – Clarification

The Sunapee Sound Project began as a technical demonstration showcasing our vision for Fenton’s Landing. It was never a fundraising campaign; no donations were received, and any donation-related elements were part of the demonstration and not active.

We shared this prototype privately with a few individuals simply to communicate our ideas. Unfortunately, the demo remained online longer than intended, which may have led to misunderstandings. We apologize for that.

We are not an active 501(c)(3) organization. After researching the feasibility of forming a nonprofit, we concluded that our area is already well served by many dedicated nonprofits. We admire and appreciate the hard work these organizations do for our community.

The Sunapee Sound Project is currently paused. We may explore it in the future with community support if it makes sense. Thank you for your understanding, and please know that we never intended to mislead anyone.
