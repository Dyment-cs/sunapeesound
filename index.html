Sunapee Sound Project – Clarification

The Sunapee Sound Project began as a technical demonstration showcasing our vision for Fenton’s Landing. It was never a fundraising campaign, nor was it ever intended to solicit or accept donations. No donations were requested or received, and any references to contributions were purely demonstrative.

We shared this prototype privately with a few individuals, including Tim Fenton, simply to communicate our ideas. Unfortunately, the demo remained online longer than we intended, which may have led to misunderstandings. For that we sincerely apologize.

At present, the Sunapee Sound Project is not an active 501(c)(3) organization. After researching the feasibility of forming a nonprofit, we determined that the area is already well served by existing charities. While we remain excited about fostering community-driven initiatives around sound, music and culture, we are pausing this project until there is clear community interest and support.

Thank you for your understanding, and please rest assured that we never intended to mislead anyone.
